How it works

Four steps from agreement to ownership. Fully automated, fully transparent.

Agree on Terms

Buyer and seller agree on the domain name, price, and currency. The arbiter is selected as a neutral third party.

Funds Locked

Buyer deposits payment into the cryptographic escrow contract. Funds are locked — the arbiter has zero access.

Domain Transferred

Seller initiates the domain transfer at the registrar. Transfer confirmation is verified on-chain via the protocol.

Release & Settle

Upon confirmed receipt, funds are released to the seller automatically. Both parties sign off. No middleman ever touches the money.

Cryptographic Protocol

Secure Transfer Protocol

Built on a multi-signature scheme where release requires consensus between buyer and seller — or arbitration by the neutral party in case of dispute. The arbiter can only resolve, never steal.

2-of-3 Multi-Signature Lock

Funds release requires any 2 of 3 parties: buyer, seller, or arbiter. The arbiter alone can never move funds.

On-Chain Transfer Verification

Domain transfer confirmation is recorded and verifiable. No manual proof needed — the protocol checks automatically.

Time-Locked Fallback

If neither party responds within the agreed window, funds are automatically returned to the buyer — no action required.

Frequently asked questions

Can the arbiter steal my funds?
+
No. The arbiter is a co-signer in a 2-of-3 multi-signature scheme. They can only sign alongside either the buyer or seller to resolve a dispute — they can never move funds unilaterally.
What happens if the seller never transfers the domain?
+
If the domain transfer is not confirmed within the agreed time window, the escrow contract automatically unlocks and the buyer can reclaim their funds. No dispute required.
Which domain registrars are supported?
+
We support all major registrars including GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare Registrar, Google Domains, and others. Domain transfer confirmation is verified automatically via registrar APIs.
Which cryptocurrencies can I use?
+
We support USDT, USDC, ETH, BTC, and other major assets. Both parties agree on the currency at the time of creating the escrow deal.
How long does a typical transfer take?
+
Setup takes under 2 minutes. Domain transfers typically complete within 24 hours depending on the registrar. Funds are released immediately upon confirmed transfer.
Is KYC required?
+
No mandatory KYC. The protocol enforces trust through cryptographic signatures, not identity verification. You control your funds and your privacy.
